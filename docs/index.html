resync

An iPhone companion for the Fujifilm X-T30 II.

recipes
Keep a library of film simulation recipes, edit them, import them from text, and write them to the camera's C1–C7 custom settings over USB-C.
geotags
The camera asks the phone for its location and time over Bluetooth, so your photos are geotagged while the phone stays in your pocket.
remote
Live view and the shutter, over the camera's own Wi-Fi.
map
See where your photos were taken and the route you walked during a shooting session.
