resync / privacy

privacy policy

effective 1 November 2026

resync has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer doesn't receive any of your data. What resync stores, it stores on your phone.

what stays on your phone

recipes
Your recipe library and anything you import.
session routes
When you start a shooting session, resync records the phone's route so it can draw it on the map.
photo places
When the camera is connected by USB-C, resync reads the location and time saved in the photos on its card to pin them on the map. It doesn't copy the photos.
settings and the log
Your settings, the camera you paired, and a log of connection events for troubleshooting.

Deleting resync deletes all of this. If you use iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, the backup includes it, as it does for any app.

what goes to your camera

resync talks only to your own camera, over Bluetooth, the camera's Wi-Fi network, or the USB-C cable. Over Bluetooth it sends the camera your location and the time when the camera asks, so the camera can write them into your photos. If you allow location "always", this continues while resync is in the background; during a shooting session iOS shows its location indicator.

the map

The map is drawn with the Mapbox Maps SDK, which downloads map tiles from Mapbox. Like other Mapbox apps, the SDK sends Mapbox anonymous usage and diagnostic data, which can include your approximate location while the map is on screen. You can turn this off at any time: tap ⓘ on the map, then Mapbox Telemetry. See the Mapbox privacy policy. If the map falls back to Apple Maps, Apple's privacy policy applies to it instead.

Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass crash reports from resync to the developer. These don't identify you.

children

resync isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions about privacy go to the issue tracker.
